Diplomat Replay

Every autosave of one game, replayed. For each turn, each European nation's diplomat scores its moves from what that nation could know; the next save shows what it really did, and the saves after that show how it turned out.

Nations

"Diplomat agrees": the move the nation made was among the diplomat's three best, or scored enough for the diplomat to make it too (small moves such as arming need 5 points, war and peace 20). "Better than usual": the ten turns after a move went better than that nation's average ten turns, by its own weights. "Wanted, not done": turns where the diplomat would have gone to war, made peace, attacked or armed, and the nation did none of these.

Turn by turn, at a glance

Each column is one turn. The bar is what the diplomat would have done (empty: wait). Marks above it are what the nation did. Hover for the turn.

Wars

Every war that broke out, who started it, and the best score the starter's diplomat gave that war in the five turns before. Above the orange line it would have declared it itself. For the King's wars, "Stay out?" says whether the diplomat would have paid the King (gold, or a boycott of its best-selling goods) to keep out of it.
