# Step 4: price agreements (decided with Mischa, 2026-10-15)

The old plan had per-turn deliveries from a named colony. Mischa: that
is hard when colonies are far apart. What players want is a **price
agreement**: "they buy my coats at 9 each for 20 turns". Used most when
your goods are boycotted in Europe: another European nation buys them
under its own Europe price and sells them there.

## The agreement

- A computer nation buys one good at a fixed price per unit for 5 to 30
  turns (default 20), as many as you bring, as often as you like.
- **By ship:** a ship of yours next to one of their coastal colonies
  with the good. On their turn they ask you in a **dialog** how many
  you sell (you may keep some for later or another buyer).
- **They can say no to a load:** each colony takes what its warehouse
  has room for (at most 200 a visit) and what they can pay. Full: "try
  another colony, or later".
- **Trust grows with every sale** (a trade memory each time).
- It ends when the turns run out, or with a war between you.

## The price

The buyer must still make a profit selling the goods in Europe:

- it agrees up to 85% of its own Europe price after tax;
- asked more, it answers with 75% (a dialog: accept or not);
- it refuses when it is boycotted for that good itself, when the good
  sells for nothing, when it does not like you (opinion neutral or
  worse) or is at war with you, or when you already have an agreement
  with it for that good.

All from its own market: honest.

## Who proposes

- **You:** Reports menu, "Post an offer", "A price agreement with one
  nation": nation, goods (with your own Europe price, or "boycott"),
  price per unit, turns.
- **The computer:** at most once every 20 turns, for a good it keeps
  needing, to the player it likes most, at 85% of what the good costs
  it in Europe.

## Fixed amounts

"100 coats for 900 within 10 turns" is the notice board WANT offer;
it can now be delivered in parts, paid pro rata.

## Seen

Offers list on the companion page: "Price agreement: buys coats at 9
each", delivered and gold earned, a bar of turns left, state
"running". Turn report: accepted, counter price, each sale, full, over.
